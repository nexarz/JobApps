Dear Hiring Committee, 

At the heart of my career lies a passion for mobilizing social change and building community resilience. With over a decade of leadership advancing anti-racism, social equity, and community safety in both municipal and national contexts, I bring a deep commitment to inclusive, cross-sector collaboration—core values that align with Strong Cities’ mission of preventing hate and extremism. It would be an honour to join your team and help amplify the Strong Cities Network in British Columbia and across Canada through the Program Manager – Canada position.

In my current role as the Inclusion Planner with the City of Richmond in British Columbia, I lead city-wide initiatives to advance ethnocultural equity and inclusion across diverse communities, including projects to combat racism and hate, foster reconciliation, and build intercultural understanding. I represent the City in several community advisory groups, where I build trusting and collaborative relationships with civil society partners and municipalities throughout the Lower Mainland. Through this work, I’ve had the opportunity to collaborate with The Foundation for a Path Forward on several initiatives, giving me a strong understanding of their role in advancing inclusive and resilient communities—an alignment I would be excited to continue in this role.

Previously, at the Vancouver School Board, I facilitated the development and delivery of my department’s first anti-racism and inclusion training, working with staff, volunteers, and partner organizations to address racial discrimination and build capacity for safer learning environments. Nationally, I served as a Board Director with YWCA Canada, where I supported our network of associations across the country in advancing gender equity and preventing gender-based violence—work that deepened my experience in stakeholder engagement and pan-Canadian capacity-building.

I am excited about the opportunity to bring my experience and energy to Strong Cities’ growing work in Canada. I believe my background in program delivery, cross-sector engagement, and policy development in municipal government, paired with my advocacy efforts across Canada, uniquely positions me to contribute to your team.

Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the opportunity to support Strong Cities’ mission to build safer and more inclusive communities across Canada.
