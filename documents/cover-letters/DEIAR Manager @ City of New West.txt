Dear Hiring Committee, 

Guided by a deep commitment to community and social impact, I have spent over a decade leading diversity, equity, inclusion, and anti-racism (DEIAR) initiatives in local government, education, and non-profit sectors. I have a strong track record of advancing racial and gender equity through policy, programs, and senior-level advocacy, with my efforts recognized locally and across Canada. My values and expertise in DEIAR will be an asset to the City of New Westminster and the broader community.

In my current role as the Inclusion Planner with the City of Richmond, I provide city-wide leadership on DEIAR initiatives, including guiding the implementation of the City's Cultural Harmony Plan to enhance intercultural harmony among Richmond’s diverse population. A key part of my work involves advising senior leadership, Council, and staff on inclusive projects and strategies that shape community development through a gender, racial, and cultural equity lens. Some notable achievements include:

Capacity Building and Education: Leading the City’s annual Diversity Symposium, Richmond’s largest DEIAR conference for City staff and community partners.

Community Engagement and Advocacy: Representing the City in its DEIAR community engagement efforts as the Staff Liaison to the Richmond Intercultural Advisory Committee and the Richmond School District’s SOGI Advisory Committee.

Strategic Leadership and Oversight: Providing subject matter expertise and evaluation research on 2SLGBTQI+ inclusion during Pride Week and beyond, informing programs, education, and policy development.

Previously, at the Vancouver School Board, I established my department’s DEIAR staff committee and led its first anti-racism and inclusion training program, engaging senior leaders and staff in trauma-informed approaches to service delivery. Beyond the public sector, I have worked extensively with nonprofit Boards of Directors and senior leaders, supporting organizations in integrating DEIAR practices into governance and strategic planning. As a former Board Director of YWCA Canada, I helped guide the organization’s national strategy on gender equity, anti-racism, and reconciliation, and chaired the Young Women’s Leadership Committee, advocating for leadership investments in young women and non-binary youth.

I am eager to bring my experience in municipal DEIAR leadership, policy development, and community-centered advocacy to lead the City of New Westminster’s DEIAR framework and strategic efforts. I look forward to discussing how my background and skills align with the needs of your team.
