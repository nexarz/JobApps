Dear Hiring Committee,

Community and social impact are at the heart of my career—mobilizing meaningful change for equity-deserving communities to thrive. With over a decade of experience leading programs in Metro Vancouver and across Canada, working with women, newcomers, youth, and seniors, I am passionate about building capacity and fostering partnerships to create resilient and connected communities. I am excited about the opportunity to bring my values and skills to BRN as the Facilitator of Programs & Partnerships.

Currently, I am a Social Policy Planner at the City of Richmond, where I provide city-wide leadership and advice on cultural equity and inclusion topics to City Council and departments, as they relate to policy planning and community development. My areas of focus include multiculturalism, anti-racism, immigration, gender equity, and inclusion for 2SLGBTQI+ communities, newcomers, and racialized groups. In this role, I lead the implementation of the City’s Cultural Harmony Plan, guiding strategies and actions on cultural equity and inclusion while building staff capacity to create a culturally inclusive community. These skills align directly with BRN’s focus on program delivery, partner collaboration, and evaluating community outcomes.

My educational background also aligns with BRN’s work. I am currently pursuing a Certificate in Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion from the University of British Columbia, which deepens my understanding of the frameworks needed to foster inclusive and equitable communities. Coupled with my Bachelor of Business Administration in Marketing and Communications, I bring expertise in program development, communication, and design strategies—key components for delivering impactful community outreach and engagement, both central to BRN’s work.

As an immigrant, my personal journey informs my passion for designing and advocating for equity-centered programs. I launched the Newcomer Youth Program at the Richmond School District, working closely with school administrators and newcomer communities to address the specific challenges faced by immigrant youth. This experience has given me a deep understanding of community needs, strategic program planning, and the importance of fostering trust in diverse groups—an area I know BRN values highly in its approach to neighbourhood resilience.

I would love the opportunity to bring my expertise to BRN and contribute to its impactful work. I hope we get the chance to work together!
