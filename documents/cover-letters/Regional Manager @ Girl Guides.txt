Dear Hiring Team,

With over a decade of experience advancing gender equity, child and youth development, and inclusive community planning in Metro Vancouver and across Canada, I’m excited to apply for the Regional Manager position with Girl Guides of Canada (GGC). I have long admired GGC’s mission of empowering young women and girls, and I bring the operational leadership, governance expertise, and values-driven approach that align closely with this role.

As the Inclusion Planner with the City of Richmond, I lead city-wide initiatives to embed equity and inclusion principles across policy planning and program and service delivery, collaborating with community partners to respond to the needs of newcomers and racialized groups. Previously, as a Community Schools Team Coordinator at the Vancouver School Board, I oversaw operations, volunteer engagement, and program administration for over 20 schools. In that role, I led cross-functional programming teams, managed budgets and grants, cultivated partnerships, and implemented operational improvements that strengthened out-of-school time programs and services to children and their families.

My journey in non-profit leadership began at YWCA Metro Vancouver, where I led engagement and advocacy initiatives focused on gender equity and youth leadership. I later served as a Board Director with YWCA Canada and co-chaired the Young Women’s Leadership and Engagement Committee, collaborating with 32 member associations across the country to advance gender equity and young women’s leadership. These leadership roles, together with my lived experience as a young racialized woman uplifted by mentorship, have shaped my lifelong commitment to empowering women and girls. They reflect my dedication to inclusive governance, meaningful collaboration, and capacity-building through a gender equity lens.

I would be proud to bring this experience to GGC—strengthening collaboration between staff and volunteers, enhancing service models, and supporting a sustainable, high-impact future for your work across the region. I look forward to the opportunity to contribute to your vision of a better world, by girls.



Peggy Chen
