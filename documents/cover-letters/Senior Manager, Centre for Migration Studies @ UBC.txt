Dear Members of the Hiring Committee,

As a newcomer to Canada who later worked in the settlement sector supporting immigrants, and now as a municipal social planner focused on immigration and newcomer inclusion, I have dedicated my career to advancing migration research, education, and outreach. These experiences have deepened my understanding of the complex dynamics of migration in British Columbia, Canada, and globally, and the importance of bridging scholarship with community engagement. In my current role, I advance the City of Richmond’s Cultural Harmony Plan by identifying strategic, city-wide opportunities for newcomer inclusion and ethnocultural equity, overseeing programs and research, and managing grants in collaboration with staff and community partners. I am excited to bring this strategic, equity-focused lens to UBC’s Centre for Migration Studies.

Some highlights of my experience that align with this role include:

Strategic planning with an EDI-D lens – Leading implementation of Richmond’s Cultural Harmony Plan and provided board-level guidance to social service organizations like YWCA Canada, embedding EDI-D principles into policies, research, and programming.

Operations, program, and financial management – At the Vancouver School Board, I oversaw program and service operations for over 24 schools, managing grants, budgets, staff and volunteer teams, and large-scale events.

Community engagement and knowledge mobilization – Designed and delivered conferences and forums, including Richmond’s annual Diversity Symposium, fostering collaboration among academics, policymakers, and social service organizations on migration, anti-racism, and intercultural dialogue.

As I further my career with a commitment to social equity for newcomers, I am eager to contribute through the intersection of academia and migration-related scholarship, drawing on my expertise in education and engagement. Having recently completed UBC’s Certificate in Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion, I bring a renewed commitment to EDI-D principles and look forward to advancing excellence in migration and mobilities research in collaboration with community partners.
