Dear Carol Nelson,

Libraries have long been a cornerstone of equity and community connection in my life. As a newcomer to Canada, the public library was one of the first places where I found a sense of belonging. Since then, I’ve had the privilege of working with libraries in multiple capacities — from facilitating programs for immigrant youth at the Richmond Public Library, to building partnerships with VPL’s children’s librarians as a Community Schools Coordinator with the Vancouver School Board, and now leading city-wide initiatives with library staff through my role as Inclusion Planner at the City of Richmond, supporting shared City and Library strategic goals.

With over a decade of experience in strategic planning, equity-driven research, and cross-sector collaboration, I’m excited to apply for the Manager, Policy & Planning role with the Vancouver Public Library. I bring a strong track record of advancing inclusive, evidence-informed strategies within public and charitable sectors, particularly for racialized, newcomer, and Indigenous communities, rooted in a deep love for libraries as a gathering place that fosters belonging, learning, and connection.

Some highlights of my experience that align with this role include:

Strategic planning, research, and evaluation: I’ve led multi-year strategy development and evaluation efforts in both municipal and education settings, including advancing the City of Richmond’s 10-year Cultural Harmony Plan, developing research briefs to inform strategic actions, and overseeing progress reports that guide policy and operational improvements across departments.

Equity-driven policy management: From guiding policy reviews to providing strategic leadership across departments to embed ethnocultural equity into policies, programs, and services, I support teams in aligning internal systems with principles of equity, diversity, accessibility, and reconciliation.

Staff management and leadership: At the Vancouver School Board, I managed a team of contract and unionized student support staff and oversaw HR functions including recruitment, onboarding, and staff development. As a current member of CUPE 718, I bring a sound understanding of collective agreements and complex unionized environments.

VPL’s commitment to reconciliation, policy innovation, and lifelong learning strongly aligns with my values. I would be honoured to bring my skills in strategic planning, inclusive policy development, and staff leadership to support the Library’s goals and the communities it serves. Thank you for considering my application!
