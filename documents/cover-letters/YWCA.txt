Hello! My name is Peggy Chen (陳怡親). I am a settler and an uninvited guest on the unceded and unsurrendered territories of the Squamish, Tsleil-Waututh, and Musqueam nations. I am an immigrant to Vancouver from Taipei, Taiwan, and I am grateful to consider both these places home. I have been a resident of the Mount Pleasant neighbourhood for close to five years, and I am passionate about facilitating place-based connections to build community inclusion and belonging. 



I would like to join the Mount Pleasant Neighbourhood House’s Community Board to strengthen relationships and cultivate a culture of collective care for the well-being of the area’s residents and community members. My hopes and dreams for a neighbourhood house are to be a space for people to connect and build a “home-away-from-home” where they are supported, valued, and included. As a member of the neighbourhood, I also look forward to getting to know my neighbours through the Community Board, and connecting with others to create change in the neighbourhood we know and love. 



I believe the MPNH Community Board would benefit from my skills and experience in community development and engagement across Metro Vancouver and in the neighbourhood, including Board governance experience with fund development and partnership opportunities. I have over a decade of work and volunteer experience facilitating programs and planning policy for and with equity-deserving groups such as newcomers, youth, seniors, and women and girls, through charitable organizations, schools, and government institutions. In my former role with the Vancouver School Board’s Community Schools Team, I worked closely with MPNH’s newcomer youth team and came to see and understand the impact of some of MPNH’s programs and activities. I have previously served as a Board director on organizations such as the Alzheimer Society of BC and the YWCA Canada, and I look forward to bringing some of these skills to the local context and growing the community I live in. 



Thank you for getting to know me and I hope we work together!
